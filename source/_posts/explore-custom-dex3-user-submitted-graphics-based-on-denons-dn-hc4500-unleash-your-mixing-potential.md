Our community is always looking for new user created and submitted skins we can include on PCDJ.com for download for DEX 3\. Different DJs have different needs and styles of course., so much of the time user submitted skins fill a niche that other DJs with similar needs gravitate to.

Our first user submitted skin comes to us from Emiliano Vargas (_DJ Vargas_) of Sweden. It’s a **1280 x 800 resolution skin** modeled after the wildly popular **Denon DN-HC4500** [DJ controller](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) and features stacked waveform’s running horizontally across the top and rotating platters that mimic those on the HC4500\.
